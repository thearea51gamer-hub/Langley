Questions owners ask

Can’t see yours? Call or text me at 250 514 8944.

What’s your commission?

A standard brokerage percentage of the selling price, plus GST, paid from the sale at closing. Nothing comes out of your pocket before then. Before you sign, I’ll show you in writing exactly what you’d net at your asking price.

Can I keep using her while she’s listed?

Yes. Most owners keep boating until there’s an accepted offer. We plan showings around your trips.

Do I have to fix things before listing?

Usually not. I’ll point out the few things worth doing because buyers and surveyors will notice them, and tell you what isn’t worth the money.

What if I change my mind?

Until you sign a listing agreement, you can stop at any point and it costs you nothing. The agreement itself explains how it can be ended, and I go through that with you before you sign.

Is now a good time to sell?

It depends on your boat. Spring is when most buyers on the Island are looking, so many owners get ready over the winter. Your valuation will show where your boat sits in today’s market.

How long will it take to sell?

It depends on the boat, the price and the season. Realistically priced boats sell fastest, and spring and early summer are the busiest months here. Your valuation includes my honest view on timing.

Does my boat have to be at Van Isle?

No. I sell boats wherever they’re kept on Vancouver Island and in the Gulf Islands.

Do I need to be there for showings?

No. I show the boat and answer buyers’ questions, and once there’s an accepted offer I run the sea trial. You’re welcome to come along whenever you’d like to.

Can I sell a boat that still has a loan on it?

Yes. The loan is paid out from the sale at closing, and I coordinate that with your lender.

Do you sell sailboats too?

Yes. I sell power and sail, all brands.

Do you handle boathouses?

Yes. I help owners sell their boathouses, and help buyers find and buy one.

I’m buying another boat too. Can you help with both?

Yes. I can sell your boat and help you find the next one, and Van Isle Marina has a trade program for owners who are changing boats. Ask me how it would work for you.

What do you need from me to get started?

Just the answers to six short questions. Her service records and any past survey help, but you don’t need them for the valuation.

Not ready to talk yet?

Get my Island market notes: a short email every so often on what’s selling here, for how much and how quickly. Useful whether you sell next spring or never.
