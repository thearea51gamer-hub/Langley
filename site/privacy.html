Privacy and terms

This site belongs to Alexander Langley, a yacht broker working with Van Isle Marina in Sidney, British Columbia. Here is what happens to the information you send through it.

What I collect

Only what you type into a form: your name, email, phone number, details about your boat or the boat you want, and how you’d like me to contact you.

How I use it

  • To prepare your valuation, answer your question or send you boats that fit.
  • To contact you the way you asked: email, a call or a text.
  • To send my monthly market notes, only if you ticked the box for them.

I don’t sell your information. Your details may be kept in Van Isle Marina’s client records so the marina can help with your boat.

How your enquiry reaches me

Forms on this site are delivered through the site’s host, Netlify, which passes them to my email. Photos you add are sent the same way.

Ads and links

If you arrive from an ad or a link, for example on Facebook or ChatGPT, the name of that ad is sent along with your enquiry so I know which ads are useful. When a valuation request is sent, the site also tells OpenAI’s ad measurement tool that a request was made, so I can see whether my ChatGPT ads work. Nothing you type in the form is shared with OpenAI or any advertiser.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time, and you can unsubscribe from the market notes from any email. Write to alangley@vanislemarina.com or call or text 250 514 8944.

Terms

Boat details and prices on this site come from the listings and can change without notice. Example valuations are illustrations only. A written valuation is an opinion of value, not an appraisal or survey.
